Flashcards

Recall beats rereading. Every chapter in the sixteen volumes has cards: its central idea, and each of its exercises with the solution on the back, 1,192 cards in all. A chapter's cards join your deck when you mark it read. Say how well you knew each answer and the card comes back just before you would forget it.

Keys: Space shows the answer, 1 2 3 rate it

Your deck

Chapters marked with a dot are in your deck. Add any chapter to start learning it before you read it, or study one chapter on its own; studying a chapter adds it. A card you know moves up a box and waits longer, 1, 3, 7, 16, 35 then 80 days; one you miss goes back to the first box. At most 20 new cards a day, so reviews never pile up. Your schedule is saved in this browser and, when you're signed in to Claude, in your account, so it follows you between devices.
